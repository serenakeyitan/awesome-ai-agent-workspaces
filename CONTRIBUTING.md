# Contribution Guidelines

Thanks for helping keep **Awesome AI Agent Workspaces** high-signal.

## What belongs here

This list is **tightly scoped** to the "Slack for AI agents" category: platforms where AI agents are **persistent teammates in a shared workspace** alongside humans and/or other agents. An entry must satisfy at least one of:

- **Multiplayer chat** — agents in channels / DMs / threads you `@mention`.
- **Shared board / canvas** — agents and humans pull from the same task queue or canvas.
- **Managed agent team** — hire / assign / budget / supervise a roster of agents.
- **Shared context layer** — a substrate giving every agent the same persistent, owned context.

## What does NOT belong here

- Single autonomous agents (Devin, AutoGPT, MultiOn).
- Multi-agent *frameworks/libraries* (CrewAI, AutoGen, LangGraph) — link them under "Related lists" instead.
- General chatbots or assistants with no team/workspace dimension.

## Entry format

One entry per line:

```
- **[Name](https://url)** 🟢 — *"Tagline."* One to two factual sentences on what it does. ([github.com/org/repo](url), LICENSE, ⭐ stars)
```

- Use the legend badges: 🟢 open source · 🟡 source-available · 🔴 closed · 🧪 experimental.
- Put the project in the section that best matches its **shape**.

## Quality bar

- **Verify before submitting.** Every factual claim must trace to a primary source — the live site, the GitHub repo, or npm. Don't repeat marketing copy you can't confirm.
- **No fluff.** Describe what the product actually does, not what it aspires to.
- **Be honest about licensing.** "Source-available" (e.g. BSL) is not "open source." Closed products are welcome — just tag them 🔴.
- **One PR per change.** Adding one project, fixing one fact — keep PRs focused and easy to review.

## Maintainer note

Entries can go stale fast in this space (stars, licenses, even whole companies change monthly). Flagging an outdated entry via PR or issue is just as valuable as adding a new one.
